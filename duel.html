                      How Duels Work

                      • Play a best-of-3 series across randomly-picked minigames (Speedrun, Eogonim, Blight, GodBot, Curse, Shrink, Mutation, Puzzle).
                      • Every round, both players get the exact same pieces/curses/GodBot decisions - only skill decides the winner.
                      • A tied round doesn't count either way. If the series is still tied after 3 rounds, one sudden-death round decides it.
                      • Disconnecting forfeits only the current round if you don't reconnect in time - the match continues.
